Mathematical Architecture

Rajasthan Royals Pressure Rating Framework (RR-PRF)

Full documentation of the data pipeline, formulas, and baseline calibrations.

1. In-Game Leverage Index (LI)

Calibrated on 95,234 chase deliveries via Logistic Win Probability (AUC: 0.896):

LI = 4 · WP · (1 - WP) · √[120 / (B_rem + 6)]

Triggered when LI ≥ 1.20, late crunch (B_rem ≤ 30 with RRR 7.5–24.0), or chase collapse (W ≥ 3 in powerplay).

2. Context Expected Values (RAE & RSPO)

Gradient-boosted machine learning predicting expected runs E[R] and wickets E[W] per delivery:

RAE_100 = [Σ(Runs - E[Runs]) / Balls] · 100
RSPO = [Σ(E[Runs] - Conceded) / Legal Balls] · 6

Measures performance relative to what an average T20 player would produce in identical conditions.

Fairness & Normalisation Controls

  • Eligibility Filter: Minimum 175 legal pressure deliveries to eliminate small-sample noise.
  • Empirical Bayes Shrinkage: Regressing noisy rates toward population priors (K = 40 balls).
  • Tier Weights: IPL matches weighted at 1.00; Full-Member Elite T20Is at 0.95; Associate matches at 0.85.
